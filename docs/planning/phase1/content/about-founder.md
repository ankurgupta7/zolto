## Draft copy (founder's voice)

**Hey — quick note before you read on.**

If you've landed on this page, you're probably not here for feature bullets.
You're here because you want to know who's behind Gwinn and whether you should
trust them with your little shop. Fair. So I'll drop the polished tone the rest
of the site has and just talk to you like a person for a minute.

**This thing started because of my wife.**

She started a small [jewelry] business on the side — nothing huge, the kind of
thing a lot of people are quietly building in the evenings. First we needed to
take card payments, so we bought one of those little [Worldline] terminals. Then
I thought, okay, let's get her a proper website too.

And that's when I hit the wall.

The companies that sell this stuff charge *a lot* of money for what are, honestly,
pretty simple things — the kind of money a small business on the side just does
not have. The terminal alone was around [CHF 400] at the very minimum. Then it
was extra to build up an inventory that the website and the counter could share
so they'd actually stay in sync. Then it was *extra again* to get API access, so
that if I wanted to build a little Android app or an iOS app of my own, I could.
Every useful thing was behind another charge.

The whole thing is tilted — and not even slightly in favor of the small business.

Here's the part that got me, though: with the AI tools that exist now — Claude,
ChatGPT, and the rest — building the thing myself turned out to be genuinely
simple. The version I made cost me [less than a dollar] to build. A dollar.
Versus hundreds up front and more every time you want to do something normal.

**So, why should you trust me with this?**

By day I'm a [research scientist] with a pretty solid computer-science background.
Over the past [fifteen-ish] years I've built some genuinely complex systems —
humbly, but the real kind — and you can go check that out on my LinkedIn
[link]. I say this not to brag but to be straight with you: at the level of
complexity I usually work at, something like Gwinn is basically entertainment. I
could put the core of it together in a few days. So this isn't a flaky side
project that'll vanish — it's an easy lift for me, built because the alternative
made me a little angry on behalf of every small maker getting nickel-and-dimed.

That's the whole story. If any of that resonates, [start your store] and let's
get you selling without the tax on being small.

— [Founder name]

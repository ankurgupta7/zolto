# Product Photography Guide for Makers

> How to take professional product photos with a phone

---

## Equipment (Under CHF 50)

| Item | Why | Cost |
|------|-----|------|
| White poster board (A1) | Clean background | CHF 5 |
| Desk lamp with daylight bulb (5500K) | Consistent lighting | CHF 15 |
| Phone tripod | Steady shots | CHF 10 |
| Reflective card (white foam board) | Bounce light, reduce shadows | CHF 5 |
| Tape + clips | Hold things in place | CHF 5 |

---

## Setup

1. **Find a window** — Natural light is best. Shoot during daytime, cloudy is ideal (soft, even light).
2. **Place poster board** — Curve it from horizontal to vertical. No visible corner.
3. **Position product** — Center, slightly angled (not flat-on).
4. **Light from side** — Lamp or window at 45° angle. Use reflective card on opposite side to fill shadows.
5. **Phone on tripod** — Eye level with product. Straight-on or slightly above (not looking down).

---

## Phone Settings

- **Grid lines:** ON (Settings → Camera → Grid)
- **HDR:** OFF (can make jewelry look artificial)
- **Flash:** OFF (creates harsh reflections)
- **Focus:** Tap on product before shooting
- **Exposure:** Adjust so highlights aren't blown out

---

## Shots Per Product

| Shot | Purpose |
|------|---------|
1. **Hero shot** | Front-facing, clean background. Main store image.
2. **Angle shot** | 45° angle. Shows depth and detail.
3. **Detail shot** | Close-up of clasp, engraving, or unique feature.
4. **Scale shot** | Product next to a coin or ruler. Customers need size context.
5. **Lifestyle shot** (optional) | Product being worn or used. Context sells.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Reflections on metal/gemstones | Diffuse light with parchment paper over lamp |
| Yellow color cast | Use daylight bulb (5500K), not warm white |
| Blurry photos | Use tripod. Tap to focus. Don't zoom (move closer). |
| Shadows too dark | Add reflective card opposite light source |
| Background not white | Adjust exposure down slightly, or edit later |

---

## Editing (Free Apps)

1. **Snapseed** (free) — Adjust brightness, contrast, white balance
2. **Canva** (free) — Crop to square (1:1) for store, 4:5 for Instagram
3. **Remove.bg** (free) — Remove background if needed (use sparingly)

**Edit checklist:**
- [ ] Straighten (grid lines help)
- [ ] Brightness: product well-lit, background clean white
- [ ] Contrast: enough to show detail, not harsh
- [ ] White balance: no yellow or blue tint
- [ ] Crop: product fills 80% of frame
- [ ] Sharpen: slightly, don't overdo

---

## Batch Workflow

1. Set up station once
2. Photograph 10 products in one session (consistent lighting)
3. Batch edit in Snapseed (copy edits, paste to all)
4. Export at 1200×1200px minimum (store standard)
5. Name files: `product-name-angle-01.jpg`

**Time estimate:** 10 products = 2 hours setup + shooting + editing

# Launch Diary #3: First Month Online

> How Sheena Arora went from Christmas markets to her first online store —  
> **Part 3: The Numbers**

---

It's been one month since Sheena Arora launched Kalakosh's online store. Time for honest numbers — not cherry-picked highlights, the full picture.

---

## The Baseline

Before the store:
- **Offline sales:** ~60/month (Christmas markets, Chilbis along the Zurich Gold Coast)
- **Online sales:** 0
- **Customer reach:** Local (whoever walks past the table)

After one month:
- **Offline sales:** ~55/month (slight dip — some regulars shifted online)
- **Online sales:** 12 orders
- **Customer reach:** Switzerland-wide (plus 2 EU orders from Germany)

**Total sales:** 67 (up from 60). Not dramatic. But the mix changed: 82% offline, 18% online. That's a start.

## Month 1 Breakdown

| Week | Online Orders | Avg Order Value | Traffic | Notes |
|------|--------------|-----------------|---------|-------|
| Week 1 (launch) | 3 | CHF 58 | 156 visitors | Instagram buzz from market customers |
| Week 2 | 2 | CHF 52 | 89 visitors | Quiet after launch |
| Week 3 | 4 | CHF 71 | 134 visitors | Instagram post about new pearl collection |
| Week 4 | 3 | CHF 62 | 102 visitors | Steady |
| **Month Total** | **12** | **CHF 61** | **481 visitors** | **2.5% conversion** |

## What Drove Sales

| Source | Orders | % of Online Sales |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| Instagram (organic) | 7 | 58% |
| Direct/returning | 3 | 25% |
| Word of mouth (links shared) | 2 | 17% |
| Search/Google | 0 | 0% |

**Search is 0% because the store has no SEO history yet.** This is expected. Month 1 is about validating the store works. Months 2–3 are about SEO and content.

## The AI Chatbot: Month 1 Stats

The chatbot handled 47 conversations in Month 1:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total conversations | 47 |
| Resolved without human help | 38 (81%) |
| Escalated to Sheena | 9 (19%) |
| Feature requests | 4 |
| Avg response time | 3.2 seconds |

**What people asked:**
- "Are the pearls freshwater or saltwater?" → AI answered from product descriptions
- "Do you ship to Germany?" → AI answered from shipping settings
- "Can I customize the chain length?" → Escalated to Sheena (custom orders)
- "The product photo doesn't match the description for the amethyst bracelet" → Bug fix deployed

## What Changed in the Product

Four features were built in Month 1, all from chatbot conversations:

| Date | Request | What Was Built | Impact |
|------|---------|----------------|--------|
| Day 3 | "Shipping cost isn't clear" | Added shipping price to product page | Reduced abandoned carts |
| Day 8 | "Can I see more angles of the pearls?" | Added zoom on product images | +15% time on product pages |
| Day 15 | "I want to buy a gift, can you wrap it?" | Added gift wrap option (CHF 3) | 3 orders used it |
| Day 22 | "The mobile menu is hard to tap" | Increased tap target size | Mobile conversion up slightly |

This is the AI-run model in practice. No backlog. No sprint planning. Just: customer asks → AI builds → deployed.

## What Didn't Work

1. **No SEO traffic yet.** The store ranks for nothing. Content (this blog series) is the fix.
2. **EU shipping is expensive.** CHF 15 to Germany killed 2 potential orders. Need to find a better shipping option or absorb some cost.
3. **The "About" page gets traffic but doesn't convert.** 40% of visitors view it, but it doesn't have a CTA. Fix: add "Shop Pearl Necklaces" at the bottom.

## The Honest Verdict

Month 1 didn't change Kalakosh's business. 12 online orders on top of 55 offline ones is incremental, not transformational.

But here's what did change:
- **Reach:** Before, zero people outside Zurich could buy. Now, 12 did — including 2 from Germany.
- **Validation:** The store works. Payments work. Shipping works. The model is proven.
- **Data:** We now know conversion rate (2.5%), average order value (CHF 61), and top traffic source (Instagram).
- **Product improvements:** 4 features built from real feedback, deployed in hours, not weeks.

Month 1 was about proving the store works. Month 2 is about proving it can grow.

## What's Next

1. **SEO content:** This blog series goes live. Target keywords: "pearl jewelry zurich," "handmade jewelry switzerland," "maker pos."
2. **Email list:** Add a "Notify me of new pieces" signup. 47 visitors engaged with the chatbot — some might want updates.
3. **Product expansion:** Add 10 more products. The 15 that launched are selling; the catalog needs depth.

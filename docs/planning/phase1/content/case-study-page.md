# Kalakosh Launch Case Study

> From 60 offline sales/month at Christmas markets to a hybrid online-offline pearl jewelry business in 30 days.

---

## The Maker

**Sheena Arora** is the founder of **Kalakosh**, a jewelry brand in Zurich. She creates handcrafted pieces in pearls and semi-precious stones — necklaces, earrings, bracelets — sold at Christmas markets and Chilbis along the Zurich Gold Coast.

Before Zolto, her entire business was offline. About 60 sales per month, all in person. She had no online store. No Instagram shop. Just a Square reader for card payments and repeat customers who found her at the same markets.

## The Challenge

The problem wasn't sales volume. It was reach and accessibility:

- Customers asked: "Do you have a website?" (answer: no)
- Each market was a new audience — no way to build ongoing relationships
- No way for existing customers to refer friends online
- Inventory was tracked mentally ("I think I have 3 of those pearl necklaces left?")

Sheena isn't a tech person. She didn't want to learn Shopify, pay for a developer, or spend hours managing software. She wanted to make jewelry.

## The Solution

Kalakosh became the first customer on Zolto — a commerce platform built for makers, with AI handling the technical work.

### What Was Set Up (3 Days)

| Day | Task | Time Spent |
|-----|------|-----------|
| 1 | Upload 15 products + AI descriptions | 3 hours |
| 2 | Configure store, shipping, payments | 1 hour |
| 3 | Sync POS inventory with online store | 30 minutes |

**Total setup time: ~5 hours.** Most of it was photography, not software.

### Key Features Used

**AI Product Descriptions**
- Uploaded product photos → AI generated descriptions
- Sheena edited for tone (5 min per product)
- Result: Professional descriptions without writing from scratch

**POS + Online Sync**
- One inventory for both channels
- Sell at a Chilbi → online stock updates automatically
- Sell online → POS knows not to oversell

**AI Chatbot**
- Handles customer questions (pearl types, shipping, sizing)
- Builds features from requests ("shipping cost wasn't clear" → fixed in 10 min)
- 81% of queries resolved without human help

## The Results (First Month)

| Metric | Before | After 30 Days |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| Offline sales | ~60/month | ~55/month |
| Online sales | 0 | 12 orders |
| Total sales | ~60/month | ~67/month |
| Customer reach | Zurich markets | Switzerland + Germany |
| Inventory tracking | Mental | Real-time sync |
| Support burden | All on Sheena | 81% handled by AI |

**Key insight:** The first online customer was someone who had met Sheena at a Christmas market three weeks earlier, lost the business card, and remembered the Instagram handle. The store exists for people who already know you — it just gives them a way to buy when you're not at a market.

## What Was Built from Feedback

In the first month, 4 features were built based on customer conversations with the AI chatbot:

| Date | Customer Said | What Was Built | Time to Deploy |
|------|--------------|----------------|---------------|
| Day 3 | "Shipping cost isn't clear" | Added shipping price to product page | 10 minutes |
| Day 8 | "Can I see more angles of the pearls?" | Added zoom on product images | 2 hours |
| Day 15 | "I want gift wrapping" | Added gift wrap option (CHF 3) | 1 hour |
| Day 22 | "The mobile menu is hard to tap" | Increased tap target size | 30 minutes |

**Total: 4 features built in 4 hours, not 4 sprints.**

## The Maker's Perspective

> "I didn't want to become a tech person. I wanted to make jewelry. Zolto let me set up a store in 3 days without learning anything new. The AI handles the questions I used to answer in Instagram DMs — like whether my pearls are freshwater or what shipping costs to Germany."
>
> — **Sheena Arora**, Founder of Kalakosh, Zurich

## What's Next

Month 1 proved the model works. Month 2 is about growth:

- **SEO content:** This case study and the Launch Diary series target keywords like "pearl jewelry zurich" and "handmade jewelry switzerland"
- **Email list:** Capture visitors who aren't ready to buy
- **Product expansion:** 15 products launched; 10 more being added

## Key Takeaways

1. **Start small.** 15 products, not 150. Launch, then iterate.
2. **Your existing audience converts first.** Online sales start with people who met you at markets.
3. **AI chatbot is a feature builder, not just support.** Customer conversations become product improvements.
4. **5 hours of setup, not 5 weeks.** If it takes longer, the tool is wrong.
